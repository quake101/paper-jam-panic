# Privacy Policy for Paper Jam Panic

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Paper Jam Panic ("the game") is made by quake101 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
information the game handles and what happens to it. In short: **the game does not collect,
send or share any personal information.** Everything it saves stays on your device.

## Information the game collects

None. The game:

- has no accounts or sign-in,
- has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind,
- does not use any third-party services or SDKs that collect data,
- does not connect to the internet (on Android it does not ask for the Internet permission
  or any other permission),
- does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file on your device that holds:

- campaign progress (stars, levels beaten, machines met, tutorial status),
- best times and Endless results,
- the Overtime scoreboard, including the name you type in when you make the board (any name
  you like; it is optional),
- Promotions and Employee Handbook perks,
- your settings (volume, screen shake, fullscreen, move stick, language and difficulty).

This file never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is not included in Android cloud
backups. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing the app's data
(on Android: Settings > Apps > Paper Jam Panic > Storage > Clear data). On Windows it lives at
`%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Paper Jam Panic\save.cfg`.

## App stores

If you download the game from an app store, such as Google Play, that store may collect
information about your download and use under its own privacy policy. We do not receive
personal information from the store beyond the anonymous, aggregated statistics stores
provide to all developers.

## Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from
anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, we will update this policy and the
date at the top before that version is released.
